# What to ask her for

Send this in groups, not all at once. **Group A blocks everything**, so ask for it first. Tick items as they arrive.
The site cannot launch until every item marked **Must** is in.

## A. Business facts (Must, ask today)

| | Ask | Format | Goes into |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ ] | Brand name as it should be written (Vastre or VASTRE) and a one-line tagline | Text | Site settings |
| [ ] | WhatsApp number that customers will message, and the one that will confirm orders | `923XXXXXXXXX` | Site settings |
| [ ] | Who confirms each order on WhatsApp, and within how many hours? **The site promises WhatsApp confirmation.** | Name and time | Real life, not the site |
| [ ] | Instagram username, and email (optional) | `vastre.pk` | Site settings |
| [ ] | Delivery charge, free-delivery amount (if any), delivery time, cities she delivers to | Rs amount, days | Site settings |
| [ ] | Which courier she uses (TCS, Leopards, Trax, M&P, own rider), and does she ship outside the big cities? | Text | Delivery policy |
| [ ] | Return and exchange rules: size exchange? how many days? who pays the courier? | Plain sentences | Return policy |
| [ ] | Cash on delivery only for now? Confirm there is no online payment | Yes / no | Checkout wording |

## B. Products (Must)

Ask for one list, in a Google Sheet or even a WhatsApp voice note typed up. For **every** product:

| | Ask | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [ ] | Product name | e.g. "Embroidered Lawn 3-Piece" |
| [ ] | Price in Rs | Whole numbers |
| [ ] | Sale price? If yes, the **old price** too | Old price shows crossed out |
| [ ] | Collection(s) it belongs to | Lawn, Formals, Winter, Kurtas... |
| [ ] | Fabric and what is in the set | "Lawn, unstitched. Shirt, dupatta, trouser." |
| [ ] | Sizes offered | XS to XXL, or none if unstitched |
| [ ] | Which sizes are sold out right now | |
| [ ] | One or two sentences about it | Her words are fine, you can tidy them |
| [ ] | Care instructions | "Dry clean only" |

Also: **How many products at launch?** Aim for 12 to 20 good ones, not 100 rushed ones. Which 4 to 8 are her best sellers, to feature on the home page?

## C. Photos and brand (Must for photos, rest Should)

| | Ask | Format |
|---|---|---|
| [ ] | **Photos of each product.** 3 to 5 each: front, back, detail, on a model or hanging. Portrait, 4:5 shape, bright even light, plain background. | JPG, at least 1200 px wide. Send as **documents** on WhatsApp (not as normal photos), because normal photos get compressed and turn blurry. |
| [ ] | One strong photo for the home page (model wearing her best piece) | Portrait JPG |
| [ ] | One cover photo for each collection | Portrait JPG |
| [ ] | **Size chart** as an image (chest, waist, length by size) | PNG or JPG |
| [ ] | Logo (the site currently writes "Vastre" in type; a logo is optional) | PNG with transparent background |
| [ ] | Brand colours she already uses, if any | Names or hex codes |
| [ ] | Her brand story: who she is, why she started, what is special about the fabric (3 to 6 sentences) | Text, English or Roman Urdu |

If she only has Instagram photos, ask for the **originals**, not screenshots.

## D. Accounts and access (Must)

| | Ask | Why |
|---|---|---|
| [ ] | **Her domain**: what is it, and where did she buy it (GoDaddy, Namecheap, PKNIC...)? Login or 10 minutes on a call. | To point the domain at the site |
| [ ] | Does she have **email on that domain**? (Zoho, Google Workspace) | So the DNS change does not break it |
| [ ] | A **Google account** she is happy to use for the order Sheet, and 20 minutes with you | Follow `ORDERS-SETUP.md` |
| [ ] | **Meta Business Manager** access, and the **Pixel ID** if she has one | Ads and tracking |
| [ ] | Her email for the Studio invitation | So she can edit the site |
| [ ] | OK to pay for hosting later if the site outgrows the free plan? (Vercel Pro is about US$20 a month, Netlify has its own paid plans.) | Avoid a surprise later |

## E. Decisions only she can make

- [ ] A launch offer (free delivery, first-order discount)? Discount **codes** are not built. A free-delivery threshold or sale prices are.
- [ ] English only, or English with some Roman Urdu in the wording?
- [ ] OK with the privacy text (it covers Meta Pixel use)? Send her the text from Site settings to read.
- [ ] Is she comfortable that customers' addresses live in a Sheet in her Google account?

## Message you can paste on WhatsApp (Roman Urdu)

> Assalam o Alaikum! Website ki tayyari shuru kar di hai. Ye cheezein mujhe bhej dein:
>
> 1. Har product ka naam, price, kis collection mein hai, fabric, kaun se sizes hain (aur kaun se abhi sold out hain), aur 1-2 line description. Sale ho to purani price bhi.
> 2. Har product ki 3-5 photos (portrait, saaf roshni). WhatsApp par "Document" ke tor par bhejein taake quality kharab na ho.
> 3. Home page ke liye ek best photo, aur har collection ki ek cover photo.
> 4. Size chart ki tasveer.
> 5. WhatsApp number jis par orders aayenge, Instagram username, delivery charges, kitne din mein delivery, aur return/exchange ki policy.
> 6. Aap ka domain kahan se khareeda hai (GoDaddy/Namecheap etc.) aur uska access.
> 7. Ek Google account (Gmail) jis mein orders ki sheet banegi, aur 20 minute ka waqt.
>
> Pehle 1 se 5 bhej dein, baaqi hum call par kar lenge.

## Order to chase her in

1. Photos and products list (the slowest by far, usually two weeks if you do not push)
2. Domain access and Google account (one 30-minute call)
3. Policies and the brand story
4. Everything else on a final call

**Do not promise her a launch date until the photos arrive.** Late photos are the most common reason a site launch slips.
