# Running your Vastre website

You change prices, products and text yourself. No code, no developer. Everything is done in the **Studio**, a private
page for you. Orders arrive in a Google Sheet.

**Your Studio:** your website address followed by `/studio`, for example `https://vastre.pk/studio`. Bookmark it. Sign in with the email you were invited with.
**Your orders:** the Google Sheet called **Vastre Orders**.

After you press **Publish**, the change is on the website within about a minute.

## Add a product

1. In the Studio, click **Products**, then the **+** (Create) button.
2. Fill in: **Product name**, then press **Generate** next to Web address, **Price**, and add **Photos** (the first photo is the main one).
3. Pick the **Collections** it belongs to, tick the **Sizes** you sell, and write a short description.
4. Click **Publish** at the bottom. If you skip this step the product stays hidden.

Photo tips: tall (portrait) photos, bright even light, plain background, at least 1200 pixels wide. Send or upload the original photo, not a screenshot.

## Change a price

Open the product, change **Price**, **Publish**.

## Put something on sale

Set **Price** to the new, lower price. Put the old price in **Old price**. The old price shows crossed out. When the sale ends, empty the **Old price** box.

## Mark something sold out

- Whole product: turn on **Sold out**, **Publish**. It stays visible but cannot be ordered.
- One size: open **Sizes that are sold out** and tick the size, **Publish**. Other sizes can still be ordered.
- Back in stock: undo the same switch, **Publish**.

## Hide a product

Open it, click the **...** menu at the bottom, choose **Unpublish**. To bring it back, click **Publish**.

## Choose what shows on the home page

Open a product and turn on **Show on the home page**. With fewer than four featured, the newest products fill the row.

## Delivery charges by city and custom sizes

In **Site settings**, **Delivery and returns**:

- **Delivery charge, or for all other cities:** for example 550.
- **City with a different delivery charge** and **Delivery charge in that city:** for example Lahore and 350. Customers who
  type that city at checkout pay the lower charge. The cart says "At checkout" until they enter their city.
- **Extra charge for a custom size:** for example 10000. Products with **Offer a custom size** turned on show a
  **Custom** size. The price goes up by this amount and the customer must type measurements at checkout. The
  measurements arrive in the Notes column of the orders Sheet.

## Change the big photos and sections on the home page

Click **Site settings**, then the **Home page** tab.

- **Big photos at the top (slideshow):** add 2 or 3 slides. Each slide has a photo, a short title, one line of text, the
  button text and which collection the button opens. After uploading a photo, click the crop icon and drag the circle onto
  the face or outfit, so it stays in view on phones and laptops.
- **Brand block:** the half-photo, half-text section in the middle of the page.
- **"Shop the look" photos:** six photos look best. For each one, pick the product it opens.

Press **Publish**. The home page updates within about a minute.

## Videos

**On a product:** open the product, find **Video (optional)** under the photos, and upload a short MP4. It plays silently on a loop as the second item in the gallery. Keep files under 10 MB. Phone recordings are usually bigger, so send those to Aazmir to shrink first.

**At the top of the home page:** **Site settings → Home page** has two fields, *Video at the top, for phones* (a tall clip) and *for laptops* (a wide clip). While a video is there, it replaces the slideshow photos, and the title and button come from the first slide. Remove the video to bring the slideshow back.

## Change delivery charge, banner, headline, WhatsApp number

Click **Site settings**. The tabs across the top group everything:

- **Store**: name, tagline, and the thin **Top banner** line (for example "Free delivery over Rs. 6,000"). Empty it to hide the banner.
- **Home page**: headline, text and the main photo.
- **Delivery and returns**: delivery charge (0 means free), free-delivery amount, delivery time, size chart.
- **Contact**: WhatsApp number like `923001234567`, Instagram, email.
- **Policy pages**: About, Delivery, Returns, Privacy.
- **Ads tracking**: the Meta Pixel number.

Press **Publish** after any change.

## Add or reorder collections

**Collections**, **+**. Give it a name, press Generate, add a cover photo, and set **Position** (1 shows first). Then open products and tick the new collection.

## Orders

Every order is a new row in **Vastre Orders** with Status **New**. Message the customer on WhatsApp to confirm, then change Status as it moves:
Confirmed, Dispatched, Delivered. Use Returned or Cancelled when needed. The colour changes by itself.

## Please do not

- **Delete Site settings.** There is only one and the site needs it.
- **Change a product's Web address after you shared its link**, because the old link will stop working.
- **Share the Studio login.** Invite people by email instead.
- **Delete rows from the orders Sheet** for real orders. Change the Status to Cancelled instead.

## If something looks wrong

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| A product does not appear | It is not published. Open it and click **Publish**. |
| A change is not visible | Wait two minutes, then refresh. |
| An order is missing from the Sheet | Ask the customer to message you on WhatsApp, then message your developer. |
